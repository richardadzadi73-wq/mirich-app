Be kind

No harassment, bullying, hate speech or threats. Do not target people for who they are.

Keep it safe

No sexual content involving minors (we report it to the authorities), no graphic violence, no self-harm promotion, no illegal goods or services, no scams, phishing, malware or spam.

Respect privacy and rights

Do not share other people’s private information or content you have no right to share. To report copyright infringement email [EMAIL] with the link, your contact details and proof of ownership.

Reporting and enforcement

Use Report on any message or user. We review reports, may remove content, restrict features or ban accounts, and will act quickly on illegal content. You can appeal by emailing [EMAIL].

Channels

Channel owners are responsible for what they publish and may be removed for breaking these rules.
